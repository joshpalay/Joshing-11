## Frozen acceptance rubric and review status

A usable-as-written outcome requires an accurate question premise and answer; an accurate explanation when present; valid answer variants; a determinate ask without an answer giveaway; requested topic and difficulty fit; material factual novelty against the relevant bank and player history; and supplied evidence for every material claim. Missing evidence is **unverifiable**, not proof of truth or falsity. The five dispositions are **usable as written**, **needs revision**, **incorrect**, **duplicate/ineligible**, and **unverifiable**. Replacements may fulfill only their original slot; the same material fact may count once across slots. Subjective appeal and difficulty remain uncertain where evidence does not settle them.

A separate Codex reviewer was explicitly assigned only the minimum raw card/source/history task and this rubric, without machine verdicts or author conclusions. Its shell failed before it accessed any private card, and an automatic approval review then failed because of a usage limit. It reviewed **0 cards**. This was an attempted separate AI review, not independent human verification. No provisional question-level review was substituted from public summaries. The missing work is review of the 22 initial soft cards, two soft replacements and the one unfulfilled slot against exact saved passages and bank/player history. Consequently no outcome is counted as verified usable here. Reviewer agent time was not metered; it is not human labor.
