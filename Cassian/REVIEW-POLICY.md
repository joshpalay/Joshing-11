# Cassian owner-approved review policy

Approved by the owner in chat on October 14, 2026. These three rules guide adjudication of the saved development material. They do not revise frozen scores or change production grading, writers, gates, bank or serving behavior.

## 1. Accept correct equivalent answers

Accept an answer that conveys the correct meaning and satisfies the actual ask, including an equivalent form absent from the listed variants. The variants list is not an exhaustive whitelist. Missing a possible correct synonym alone does not make an otherwise determinate question ambiguous or unusable.

Require exact wording when identifying that wording is the point of the question, such as a requested missing word or specified language. Distinguish a genuinely different or incomplete answer from an unambiguous name, abbreviation or paraphrase. Case judgments must establish that the proposed alternative is actually equivalent in context; this policy does not declare every proposed synonym correct.

Record explicit full-name and similar wording issues for human adjudication: determine what the ask tests and whether the accepted form satisfies it. The policy does not silently remove requirements from frozen cards. The current production grader already requests semantic leniency; this approval authorizes no prompt or grading-code change.

## 2. Separate difficulty from factual accuracy

A wrong requested tier is a reason to reclassify a correct question. Record factual accuracy, proposed tier and readiness for the requested tier separately. Retiering does not cure an unsupported explanation, invalid answer or giveaway.

Keep all original request and shortage denominators. A correct card that needs another tier is potential stock for that tier, not automatically a fulfilled original request. Do not raise the as-written requested-slot yield merely by changing the difficulty label after the outputs.

## 3. Revise meaningful answer giveaways

Revise a stem that reveals a meaningful part of the answer, including an effectively equivalent phrase. A shared subject or entity alone does not disqualify a question. Assess whether the stem provides what the player was supposed to retrieve.

A prior bank card mentioning the answer to another, materially different question is a separate history/round-composition consideration. It is not automatically a duplicate fact or a giveaway inside the current stem. That serving-policy issue remains unresolved by these three approvals.
