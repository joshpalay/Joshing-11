# Cassian: bounded coverage integration

October 12, 2026. This is a separate opt-in test of the [offline coverage repair](COVERAGE-REPAIR.md), following merged #1786 at `d3fe6904`. It preserves all earlier code, inputs, gold and scores. Its purpose is to verify real-model integration and the cost of added checking, not production reliability or new usable-question economics. Actual findings are reported separately in [coverage results](COVERAGE-PROBE-RESULTS.md).

Two previously approved packets produce four Haiku 5.5 writes, soft/strict per topic, alternating arm order. Both writer inputs pass the source-completeness guard before jobs exist. The empirical packet uses the separate six-source revision; the other packet has an explicit required-relationship/qualifier plan. Required components and their fact-bound passages reach the writer. Parent captures and audit identities do not.

Source reconstruction uses Sonnet 5.5 and receives the entire card, every variant, readable sources and the acceptance policy, without writer inventory or gold. The new native schema asks for each atomic material assertion's evidence basis. Indirect recommendations/subject mentions cannot mechanically establish either support or contradiction; those statuses become unknown in an explicit projection used by the established quote/binding checks. Raw responses remain untouched. The model can still mislabel a basis, omit material claims or reason incorrectly; a direct label is not proof.

Strict cards with structurally valid inventories and source replies receive a separate Sonnet 5.5 reconciliation call. It sees full card context and both assertion sets, without prior decisions, arm/gold labels or source verdicts. Its complete mapping must bind same-field writer IDs to every reconstructed claim. Code stamps the reviewer metadata; the model cannot claim independent human adjudication. Catalogs and dependent requests bind actual native source/writer receipt hashes, not only the transformed projection. Source/quality failures cannot be erased by a matching inventory.

Two extra reconciliation controls use a saved source-reviewed card: the original incomplete inventory and a version adding its audited missing explanation record. Shorter identifying quote anchors and existing paraphrases remain. Card text, source assertions and quotes are retained, with new opaque IDs. These are explicitly author-prepared saved-response projections, not fresh source-model replies. The pair tests both omission detection and retention of a complete inventory; rejecting everything cannot pass.

Eight source/quality controls retain the prior frozen text and gold: two sound, three challenge and all three chronology/attribution/formation regressions. Actual model-visible IDs share one opaque format. Quality sees only question, canonical answer, domain and tier; generated cards and controls share two hash-ordered batches. No model receives owner ratings, private cohorts or production player identities/history.
