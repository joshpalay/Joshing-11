## Separate reason audit

This is **same-author development adjudication**, not independent human validation. All source/quality judgments and all 24 reconciliation rows were reviewed against complete saved cards and source passages. Original gold, raw responses and scores are unchanged.

The saved inventory pair correctly preserves the same-field rule: a record in the question or answer cannot cover an explanation omission. All twelve mappings across this pair have sound reasons. Short identifying quotation anchors do not trigger the old containment false alarms.

The strict music block has a real coverage basis: the question explicitly supplies a scale-size qualifier that its question inventory does not record. Its other four mappings preserve the relationships. The empirical block is mixed. The source reviewer expands the question's trial name into additional design/intervention details that the question never states; the reconciliation checker inherits this expansion and reports a missing record. That is not a proven writer omission. Two variant records do omit the reconstructed outcome/exposure relationship under the frozen field contract. Yet the answer record, which omits the same qualifier, is marked covered by referring to context in other fields. That is inconsistent with the same-field rule. Literal IDs and quote locations cannot detect this semantic inconsistency.

The stricter evidence basis prevents explicitly indirect recommendations from proving empirical results. In this run the revised full passages contain the direct results, and the checker uses them. Some cited fragments still omit part of the asserted relationship even though the complete supplied passages support it. This is incomplete cited evidence, not demonstrated card falsity. Native-format and literal-quotation success do not establish semantic support.

The formation regression passes again. Its source describes two predecessors forming a new league; the checker accepts an explanation describing one predecessor merging into that league. Historical first-game continuity is a separate reasonable convention and does not settle the precise formation relation. Preserve the frozen gold and report this sensitivity separately.

The strict empirical quality hold cites specialist knowledge as ambiguity. At the frozen specialist tier the named trial and outcome give a determinate ask; this is a policy disagreement, not proof of multiple answers. Independent difficulty/appeal adjudication remains unassigned. Chronology, attribution, answer-type, explicit full-name and giveaway controls otherwise have sound reasons, with the chronology language variant separately rejected.
