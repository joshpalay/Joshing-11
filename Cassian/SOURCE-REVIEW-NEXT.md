# Cassian next source-review comparison

Design proposal, October 14, 2026. The [saved-output reconciliation](RECONCILIATION.md) is complete as an informed development assessment. This document makes the next decision concrete; it is not a frozen dispatch plan or authorization for a new production path. No paid comparison is ready yet.

## Question to answer

Can one source-review stage establish support for every material card claim, including valid answer forms, at lower full creation cost per usable original request, without increasing unsafe passes or hiding unresolved evidence? Source review accounted for $0.488242 of the saved soft arm's $0.5410001 provider estimate.

Evaluate a replacement for that stage before adding any further checker layer. The zero-saving pre-check and manually authored semantic bindings are not evidence of an economical automated solution.

## How review would work

1. Freeze each writer output, supplied source packet and captured novelty candidates. Give the reviewer the actual question, answer, explanation and variants, not a writer inventory treated as truth.
2. Have one source reviewer reconstruct material claims and identify entity roles, required qualifiers, answer context and predicate meaning. Require a supported, contradicted or unresolved assessment for each claim with source identity and exact evidence. Report missing coverage explicitly. A real quotation alone is not proof it supports the claimed relation.
3. Use the existing offline verifier for mechanical binding, exact quotations, same-field coverage and malformed-output checks. It must reject invented evidence or silent claim repairs. Its mechanics cannot certify that reviewer-extracted identities or predicates are correct.
4. Apply quality and novelty decisions separately, retaining giveaway, determinacy and requested baseline-tier failures. Record player-specific fit independently. An unresolved case remains unresolved; an optional human exception path must be explicitly staffed and costed, not assumed free.
5. Audit complete cards, including explanations and variants. Keep all failures, repairs and replacements in the original-request denominator. A corrected card receives its own version and review; one request contributes at most one successful outcome.

This is a proposed evaluation path. No production prompt, grader, model choice, database, serving rule or existing frozen verifier policy changes here.

## Evaluation and accounting

Use the saved 24 outputs and 31 related calibration cases for development and regression checks only. They have been repeatedly seen; neither a fresh reviewer nor reshuffling makes them unseen examples.

Before any paid run, freeze genuinely unseen requests and source/history inputs, a consistent general-adult review audience, policy-sensitive exceptions, and evidence-backed reference judgments. Group related facts and shared sources together when separating development and evaluation. Record who establishes the reference judgments and their prior exposure; independent adjudication is preferred, but none is assigned by this proposal. If unavailable, retain that limitation and do not claim independently verified yield.

Specify exact reference and candidate prompts/schemas, models, common transports, repetition/retry rules, output ceilings, full provider quote and an explicit affordable run cap. Historical broken source replies remain historical failures; do not silently repair them and claim a successful baseline. A future working reference path must be separately named, priced and measured.

Compare full-card verdicts and reasons with the reference, including unsafe passes, false holds, unresolved cases and required claim coverage. Difficulty and answer-policy disagreements receive their own labels. A correct verdict for the wrong reason is not semantic success. Include positive examples that would reveal indiscriminate holding as well as genuine challenge cases. Freeze numeric tolerances before dispatch rather than selecting them after results.

For each operating path record:

- Provider dollars for retrieval, writing, source review, quality, malformed replies, retries, repairs and replacements, including failures.
- Active preparation, evidence extraction, adjudication and exception-review minutes, with unknown values left unknown. Distinguish evaluation-only reference work from recurring operating work; do not hide either.
- New unique usable questions fulfilling the original requested tier, out of all requests and shortages. Retiered stock is a separate secondary inventory.
- End-to-end readiness for cold/depleted requests and captured-history coverage; call duration and curated-source setup do not establish these.

The primary provider ratio is total operational provider dollars divided by independently verified new usable requested-slot outcomes. Report labor separately, or show total cost only with an explicit labor rate and measured time. Zero independently verified keeps leave unit cost undefined. The historical source-review savings ceiling is $0.488242 for this saved cohort; $0.0527581 of other provider charges and all unknown labor remain even if source review were free. Savings must be recomputed when yield changes.
