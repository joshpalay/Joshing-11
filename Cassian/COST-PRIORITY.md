## Primary measurement

Divide all operational creation costs by the number of new, unique questions independently verified as usable for the requested topic, difficulty and history constraints. Include retrieval/evidence preparation, writing, all checks/escalations, repairs, failures and replacement attempts. Failed or unresolved topics retain their costs; duplicate, unverified, incorrect or already-seen facts do not enter the usable denominator. If no usable questions result, report zero yield and cost per usable question as undefined.

Report provider dollars and active preparation/adjudication labor minutes separately; unmeasured labor is unknown, not free. Any combined labor-dollar estimate must state its assumed rate. Separate experiment-only control/audit overhead from a modeled operating creation path, while retaining all of it in actual experiment spending. Use matched inputs and disclose shared-retrieval allocation when comparing arms.

Quality, answer-variant validity, novelty and narrow-topic fulfillment remain constraints. A lower writer token bill or more permissive checker is not enough if failures/replacements erase the saving. The weekly report's generation spend per saved row remains a different proxy; bank copies are not new question creation.
