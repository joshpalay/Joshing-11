## Inventory reconciliation

[`cassian-claim-reconciliation.ts`](../scripts/cassian-claim-reconciliation.ts) builds content-bound identities for writer and independently reconstructed claims. It binds the whole card, packet and exact reviewer response. Source reconstruction still receives the whole card without the writer inventory; a separate reconciliation input supplies actual card context and the two sets of assertions, excluding source verdicts, arm labels and gold.

Only identical assertions in the same field match automatically, allowing harmless whitespace/quote typography. There is no fuzzy matching, quote-containment test, shared-fact-ID shortcut or cross-field coverage. Paraphrases stay uncertain until a complete explicit audit links the appropriate same-field writer IDs. One reconstructed relationship may require several records. Missing claims, uncertain equivalence and confirmed coverage are distinct. Incomplete, duplicate, stale or cross-field audit records fail validation.

## Source completeness

[`cassian-source-completeness.ts`](../scripts/cassian-source-completeness.ts) requires a bound plan for every eligible prepared fact. The plan explicitly lists its relationships and qualifiers, direct-support judgments, captured parent identities and actual parent quote ranges. Code can locate unique parent quotes without model offsets. It validates excerpt provenance, source/fact identities, statement hashes and whether each required passage is both supplied and bound to the prepared fact's evidence.

Unknown or contradicted claims, incomplete decomposition, absent quotations and support labeled as a recommendation/subject mention block preparation. These categories remain separate. Selecting the label `direct_relationship` or attesting completeness is still a judgment; the program cannot prove semantic entailment from that label. Independent source truth, novelty and current-player eligibility remain unassessed.

The saved empirical packet is rejected for clipped required result passages. A **new revision** adds two intact contiguous passages from the existing captured parent, increasing supplied sources from four to six. It preserves the original fact statements, support/novelty labels and all original source bytes. Required evidence is also bound to the facts and included as explicit components in checked writer input. Rebuilding a complete packet is idempotent. Distant passages are never joined into a fabricated quote, and unresolved claims cannot be repaired by inventing evidence.

The saved recommendation-based waiver is rejected under its explicit indirect-support classification. This is an offline adjudication of the evidence basis; no model has yet demonstrated that it will classify the basis correctly itself.
