## Fresh comparison: 24 topic requests

Use 24 previously untested topic/history targets: eight broad, eight niche and eight very narrow interests. Within each breadth group select four cold-source requests and four depleted-stock requests; each group of four has one accessible, two moderate and one specialist request. Cold means no usable source cache at the selection snapshot. Depleted means no eligible unseen bank card for that user/history snapshot; it can still have cached sources. Record both states rather than treating them as mutually exclusive features of the topic itself.

Build an eligible frame from real requested interests, actual tiers and frozen exposure/bank histories. Exclude prior experiment targets and every fact already seen by the selected player; do not remove difficult or apparently empty topics. Assign breadth/state labels before retrieval or writer outputs, ideally by a person other than the prompt author. Sort eligible targets within each cell by SHA-256 of `cassian-v3-followup-2026-10-12:<target-id>` and take four. If a cell lacks four, record the shortage and freeze a reduced denominator; never fill it by broadening a narrow interest. Freeze frame hash, selection, exclusions, player-history hash and all requested tiers before outputs.

Compare the same Haiku writer with soft versus v3 strict evidence instructions, alternating order. Both receive the same retrieved source bytes and eligible fact/history constraints; source preparation and review policy are common factors. This isolates instructions, not model choice. Keep one initial attempt per arm/request; permit at most one predeclared repair/replacement per arm, record its exact trigger, and count both attempts and spending. Budget exhaustion or unsupported-fact shortage remains an unresolved topic request. Preserve the originally selected request even when no usable card emerges.

Keep the three known relation regressions as mandatory development checks, separate from the fresh sample. Add a separately selected control set with at least eight sound cards and eight challenge cards spanning contradiction, absent evidence, quotation formatting, answer acceptance and quality defects. Freeze labels/evidence independently of model outputs. Report sound-card retention and unsafe passes separately; a reject-everything checker must fail the retention comparison. Prompt-author controls cannot be called independent or unseen validation.

Where feasible, a person other than the prompt author adjudicates every material assertion, variant and reviewer reason from source captures, blind to arm and model decisions. Record reviewer identity/role, disagreement and time. If unavailable, disclose same-author adjudication and treat findings as development evidence; do not manufacture independence or a rollout conclusion. Any policy-sensitive exclusion is reported alongside the full frozen result, not used to replace it.

Report each arm's verified novel usable cards per original request, unresolved/shortage requests, contradictions, missing evidence, malformed citations/output, incomplete inventories, incorrect variants, quality errors and sound-control retention. Keep pass/hold/review separate. Compare paired outcomes and show every breadth/state/tier cell, not just survivors. No numerical reliability or monthly-savings claim follows from a small test. Zero known regression escapes is a necessary development condition, not sufficient rollout evidence.

## Measure the complete production task

Start the readiness clock when the real topic/history request is fixed, before retrieval. Record per-attempt retrieval/capture, source extraction, fact-inventory preparation, writing, all checks, escalation, adjudication, repairs, failed calls and replacements. Use a trace linking selected request → every attempt → final eligible serving candidate or unresolved outcome. Count source preparation once at request level, split shared source expense equally between arms only for the paired comparison, and disclose the allocation.

Record provider-response costs and ledger settlements separately from actual invoices. Include unknown charges as reserved ceilings until reconciled. Record active evidence-preparation and adjudication minutes prospectively, automation compute time, and operator blocking time. Do not silently assign unmeasured manual labor zero cost. Report both provider dollars and labor minutes; any labor-dollar sensitivity must state its hourly-rate assumption. Manually curated packets are an experimental dependency whose cost and production feasibility must be reported.

Measure actual cold-source readiness and depleted-topic readiness separately from a cached-source replay. Report median/tail time among successes **and** time spent plus censoring reason for every unresolved request; a success-only latency median can hide failure. Do not substitute summed model durations or assumed parallelism for observed topic-to-ready wall time. Preserve requested scope, difficulty and no-repeat constraints through failures and repairs.

Before rollout, measure the intended operational process with those traces, including automatically prepared evidence or the labor dependency it actually needs. Reconcile the provider bill, fixed workload and monthly estimate. Require independent evidence review where feasible, usable novelty/coverage, acceptable false-hold/unsafe-pass behavior and an easy rollback before any live writer switch. This protocol does not modify serving or authorize a model/prompt deployment.
