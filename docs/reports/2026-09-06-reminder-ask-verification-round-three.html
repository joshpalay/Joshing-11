What else the walkthrough turned up

Overclaim · activity copy

“You’ve got Renaissance Florence down cold” — on two correct answers, at the lowest tier. Home said it on a first-ever session. PLAYER_MASTERY for that territory reads tier=establishing, tier_reached_at=null, 60 points; the session recap on the same data says NEXT: FAMILIAR. Two surfaces, opposite claims, same moment.

It is one of six lines in the you_got pool in activity-stream.ts, chosen by hash of the event id. The other five describe the event that happened (“You knew {friend}’s {topic} question”); this one alone asserts a standing fact about the player’s knowledge that the event does not evidence. Same shape as the “First five complete” eyebrow #1610 just fixed — copy asserting a state its gate never checks. Cheapest fix is to retire the line from the pool.

Mislabel · question badge

The player’s first two questions were badged “General Knowledge”, not “Renaissance Florence”. Both slots carry domain: "Renaissance Florence" — the topic they had just chosen — and both display the generic bucket instead, on the screen whose promise is “Made from your topics, not pulled off a shelf.”

Not a data error at the slot level: questionBadges in daily/page.tsx prefers broad_category, then category, and only falls back to domain — least specific first. 68 of 1,302 questions with a specific canonical_subcategory carry “General Knowledge” as their broad category (5.2%), and every one of them badges generically. Hitting two of two on a first session was luck; the exposure is real. Either reorder the fallback, or stop letting a specific subcategory sit under a generic broad category.

Leak shape · outside #1611’s reach

The first question a new player saw glossed its own answer. “Literally meaning the offices, what famous Florentine museum…” — answer: The Uffizi, which is what uffizi means. The stem hands over a translation of the answer word while withholding the word.

It is answer_source: creator_written, so it never passed through the generator at all. The authored path calls textContainsAnswer, a containment check the stem passes cleanly; the partial-leak gate #1611 shipped lives in generate-questions.ts and does not run here. Worth deciding whether the new gates belong on the authored path too — the leak family is identical, and a friend-authored question is exactly what a new player meets first.

What this says about the process

Three rounds, three different failure modes

Round one found the toast never rendered. Round two found the fix rendered it under a takeover layer. Round three found it working — and to say so with any confidence needed an instrument, not a look, because the thing being verified lives for 1.8 seconds and the previous round’s failure was invisible by construction.

The general lesson is narrow but worth keeping: when the defect is “the player never saw it”, the verification has to measure presence and coverage, not take a screenshot and hope to catch it.

The flow under test was the least of it

The reminder ask passed cleanly. Everything worth Josh’s attention above — a mastery claim on two answers, a topic badged as its own opposite, an answer glossed in its own stem — came from walking past those screens on the way, on a genuinely new account. None of it is reachable from a test suite, and none of it was what the walkthrough was for.
